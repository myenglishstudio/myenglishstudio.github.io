Sentence Transformation Practice

Rewrite the outcome in each sentence using managed to + bare infinitive (for successful actions achieved despite difficulty) or failed to + bare infinitive (when someone did not succeed in doing something).

Example 1: "After waiting in a queue for 3 hours, I bought tickets to the football match."
➔ I managed to buy tickets to the football match.

Example 2: "Despite trying all afternoon, he couldn't reach customer service on the phone."
➔ He failed to reach customer service on the phone.
