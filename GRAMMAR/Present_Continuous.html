Present Continuous Practice

Task 1: Positive and Negative Forms (Set 1 - Easiest)

Instruction: Complete the present continuous sentences using positive forms or negative forms (e.g., I'm working / She isn't working).

Task 2: Positive and Negative Forms (Set 2)

Instruction: Make the present continuous using contractions or full forms.

Task 3: Present Continuous Questions (Set 1)

Instruction: Form correct Yes/No and Wh- questions in the present continuous tense (e.g., Are you eating...? / Where are you going...?).

Task 4: Present Continuous Questions (Set 2)

Instruction: Create Yes/No or Wh- questions from the prompts given.

Task 5: Present Continuous All Forms (Exercise 1)

Instruction: Identify whether the prompt is a positive statement, negative statement, or question and form the present continuous accordingly.

Task 6: Present Continuous All Forms (Exercise 2)

Instruction: Practice mixed forms (statements, negatives, and questions) with short or long forms.

Task 7: Present Continuous All Forms (Exercise 3 - Most Challenging)

Instruction: Complete these advanced mixed present continuous sentences.
