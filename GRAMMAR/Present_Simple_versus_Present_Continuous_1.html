Quick Grammar Reference

Both full forms (do not, is doing) and contractions (don't, 's doing) are accepted
Present Simple (do / does)

Habits and routines (always, usually, twice a week, every day), permanent facts, timetables, and stative verbs (know, like, see, think).

Present Continuous (am / is / are + -ing)

Actions happening right now (now, right now), temporary situations (this week, this month, today), changing trends, and complaints with always.
