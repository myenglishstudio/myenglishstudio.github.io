Duration Structure Practice

Rewrite each sentence using It takes (someone) [time] to do smth (general/routine facts) or It took (someone) [time] to do smth (completed past events).

Example 1: "I spent forty-five minutes preparing the quarterly slides yesterday."
➔ It took me forty-five minutes to prepare the quarterly slides yesterday.

Example 2: "A passenger normally spends twenty minutes getting through security."
➔ It takes twenty minutes to get through security. (or: It takes a passenger twenty minutes...)
