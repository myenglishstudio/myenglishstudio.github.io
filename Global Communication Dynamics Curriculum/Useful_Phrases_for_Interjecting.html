Useful Phrases for Interjecting

Select the phrases you plan to practice or use in discussions.

Seeking Information & Clarity

  • "Sorry to jump in, but could you clarify..."
  • "Just to make sure I’m following..."
  • "Before we move past that point, can I ask..."
  • "Sorry, quickly—what did you mean by..."

Contributing & Expressing Views

  • "If I could add a quick perspective on that..."
  • "Can I just jump in with an alternative view here?"
  • "To build on what you're saying..."
  • "I hear you, though I’d argue that..."

Conversation Management

  • "Forgive the interruption, but in the interest of time..."
  • "Mind if I steer us back to..."
  • "Before we go too far down this path, let's..."
  • "Sorry to cut across, but to keep us on track..."

Psychological & Behavioral Drives

  • "Can I stop you right there before I lose this thought?"
  • "Sorry, I just need to get this out while it's fresh..."
  • "Not to take over the floor, but..."
  • "I know I’m cutting in, but I couldn't resist saying..."
