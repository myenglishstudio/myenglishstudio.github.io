Google Interview Phrases Practice

Interactive exercises to master professional, time-buying phrases for your recruiter screen.

Task 1: Phrase Matching Exercise

Click a phrase on the left, then click its corresponding completion/purpose on the right to match them.

Task 2: Sentence Builder

• Click a word in the word bank below to add it into your sentence.
• Click-hold-and-drag words inside the top box to position them exactly where you want (a vertical line shows where it will land!).
• Double-click a word in the sentence box to remove it back to the bank.

Task 3: Fill in the Missing Words

Type each missing word into its box and click "Check Answer" under each sentence. You have 2 attempts per sentence before the answer is revealed!
