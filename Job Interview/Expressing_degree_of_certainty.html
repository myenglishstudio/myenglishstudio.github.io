Modal Verbs of Deduction for Business Analysts

Practice expressing degree of certainty (Must = 100%, Could = 50%, Might = 30%) and temporal context (Present vs. Past).

Task 1: Infinitive Form Selection (Simple vs. Perfect)

Complete the assumptions by writing the correct infinitive form of the verb in parentheses (e.g., be, be experiencing, or have crashed).

1. Look at the real-time metrics! Traffic dropped to zero two minutes ago. The main gateway server must (crash) right now.
2. Yesterday's revenue drop was sudden. The payment integration team might (deploy) a broken update during peak hours.
3. The bounce rate is unusually high today. Users could (find) the new checkout layout confusing at the moment.
4. The server migration completed last night without errors. The database must (run) on the new infrastructure now.
5. The competitor launched their campaign three weeks ago. That marketing decision might (cause) our user churn last month.
6. Why is CPU usage at 99%? A rogue background job must (consume) all system memory right now.
7. Conversion rates dropped during the holiday weekend. A third-party API outage could (block) international payments while we were offline.
8. The API response time is consistently under 50ms today. The engineering team must (optimize) the query parameters already.
9. I see user engagement spiking right now. Our target audience might (react) positively to the push notification sent 5 minutes ago.
10. The system logs from Q2 are incomplete. An unannounced bug might (corrupt) the analytics tables last quarter.

Task 2: Select the Modal Verb of Deduction

Choose must (100% certainty based on facts), could (50% logical possibility), or might (30% weak possibility) based on the context clues.

1. [Context: Log files confirm 0 successful database connections.]
The authentication server be completely down; there is no other explanation.
2. [Context: It's a plausible theory, but we only have 50/50 confidence.]
The drop in engagement be related to the regional network outage, but we need more data to confirm.
3. [Context: Remote possibility; low confidence scenario (30%).]
A minor holiday in a small target market have caused this slight dip in traffic, though it's unlikely to be the primary reason.
4. [Context: All 500 error logs point directly to the broken payment gateway release.]
The engineers have deployed a buggy payload; the error stack proves it.
5. [Context: Moderate possibility; standard technical troubleshooting hypothesis.]
The latency spike be caused by unindexed database queries.
6. [Context: Long-shot scenario; faint possibility.]
An ad-blocker update be interfering with tracking scripts, but we haven't seen reports of this elsewhere.
7. [Context: Ingestion pipeline metrics show zero incoming records since 9:00 AM.]
The data pipeline have stalled this morning; no new records are being written.
8. [Context: Reasonable alternative explanation under review.]
The conversion improvement be a result of the simplified navigation menu.
9. [Context: Remote, speculative edge case.]
A minor ISP DNS glitch be affecting a tiny fraction of users in one city.
10. [Context: Undeniable proof—active subscriptions dropped immediately after billing change.]
The price increase have driven away budget-conscious subscribers.
