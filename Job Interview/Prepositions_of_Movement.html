Prepositions of Movement Drill

Analyze the chart data and complete the sentences using the correct prepositions (to, by, at, from, around).

Quick Preposition Reference:
  • TO = The final destination/endpoint (e.g., dropped to 10%).
  • BY = The amount of change/difference (e.g., dropped by 5% [from 15% to 10%]).
  • AT = A specific fixed point or peak (e.g., peaked at 30%).
  • FROM / TO = The starting point and endpoint (e.g., rose from 10% to 25%).
  • AROUND / HOVERED AT = Fluctuating near a value (e.g., hovered around 15%).

Chart 1: Customer Churn Rate

1. In Q1, the churn rate started at 12 per cent and then decreased _____ 8 per cent in Q2.
Correct Answer: to
Explanation: 8 per cent is the final level/destination at Q2.
2. Between Q1 (12%) and Q2 (8%), the churn rate dropped _____ 4 percentage points.
Correct Answer: by
Explanation: 4 percentage points represents the amount of difference (12 - 8 = 4).
3. In Q3, the churn rate surged _____ a peak of 18 per cent.
Correct Answer: to (or peaked at)
Explanation: "Surged to a peak" or "peaked at 18 per cent" marks the highest point reached.

Chart 2: Landing Page Conversion Rate

4. Following the redesign in Month 2, conversion rates grew _____ 2.5 per cent _____ 5.5 per cent in Month 3.
Correct Answer: from ... to
Explanation: Identifies the starting point (2.5%) and the ending point (5.5%).
5. Month 3 saw a major lift, where conversion rates increased _____ 3 percentage points.
Correct Answer: by
Explanation: 3 percentage points is the size of the gain (5.5 - 2.5 = 3.0).
6. From Month 4 onwards, conversion rates stabilized and hovered _____ 5.8 per cent.
Correct Answer: around (or hovered at)
Explanation: Used when performance remains roughly level around a central value.

Chart 3: Monthly Operational Expenses ($k)

Explanation: Use "at" after verbs like peaked, plateaued, or stabilized to state a specific figure.
8. Thanks to automation, expenses fell _____ $35,000 between May ($95k) and July ($60k).

Explanation: $35,000 is the total savings/reduction gap ($95k - $60k = $35k).
9. By August, spending decreased _____ a new baseline of $42,000.
Correct Answer: to
Explanation: $42,000 represents the final value reached.
