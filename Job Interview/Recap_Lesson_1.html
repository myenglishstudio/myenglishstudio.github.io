Task 3: Deconstruct the XYZ Formula

Remember the Google XYZ Formula: Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y] by doing [Z].
X: The Business Impact (What you achieved)
Y: The Measurement (The numbers, data, or metrics)
Z: The Action (The tools, skills, or coding languages used)

Read the isolated snippets below and identify if it is an X, Y, or Z.
1. "...by building dynamic interactive dashboards in Tableau."
Is it X, Y, or Z?
It is Z (The Action/Tools).
Explanation: This describes the technical skill and exact action taken to solve the problem.
2. "...resulting in a 15% drop in customer churn rate."
Is it X, Y, or Z?
It is Y (The Measurement).
Explanation: This is the quantifiable metric that proves the success of the work.
3. "Boosted quarterly marketing ROI..."
Is it X, Y, or Z?
It is X (The Accomplishment).
Explanation: This is the high-level business impact or achievement.
4. "...as measured by saving the team 10 hours of manual labor per week."
Is it X, Y, or Z?
It is Y (The Measurement).
Explanation: Time saved is a classic "Y" metric to quantify efficiency.
5. "Streamlined the legacy data reporting pipeline..."
Is it X, Y, or Z?
It is X (The Accomplishment).
Explanation: This highlights the overarching goal that was achieved.
