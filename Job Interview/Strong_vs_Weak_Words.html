Part 1: Action Verbs & Responsibilities
Not-So-Good Word Choice Why It Weakens Your Impression Senior/Professional Upgrade
"Worked on..." Completely vague. Doesn't say what you actually did or delivered.
"Helped with..." Signals that you weren't leading or accountable for the outcome.
"Supported..." Sounds like administrative assistance rather than analytical ownership.
"Assisted in..." Passive phrasing that minimizes your contribution.
"Looked at data..." Sounds informal, untrained, and passive.
"Made a dashboard..." Sounds like a basic high school project.
"Ran SQL queries..." Running queries is a basic task, not an achievement.
"Fixed the data..." Vague—doesn't explain what was wrong or how you fixed it.
"Pulled reports..." Makes you sound like a human copy-paste button.
